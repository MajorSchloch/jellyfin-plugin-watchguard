# WatchGuard v0.7.13 — seamless item navigation

- Open a movie or series from Watchlists without a brief view of Home while Jellyfin loads the item.
- Keep a lightweight loading state until the matching native detail view is ready, then retain native destination focus.
- Recover from cancelled or failed navigation, Back/Forward, logout and account changes without leaving a stale loading cover.

The patch preserves Chromium 79 support, native history, watchlist filters, permissions and deletion protection. Physical LG TV validation remains necessary.
