# WatchGuard v0.7.17 — consistent TV focus

- Match the native TV menu’s text growth when Watchlists has focus, including after activation.
- Follow the rounded Series viewing heading with one continuous focus indicator, whether the section is closed or open.
- Keep keyboard focus visible in forced-color themes.
- Keep focus on the current control as you move into the section, with existing arrow and Back behavior.
