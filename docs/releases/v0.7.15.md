# WatchGuard v0.7.15 — return to your place in Watchlists

- Return from a movie or series to the selected title and approximate scroll position in Watchlists.
- Restore the previous keyboard or TV remote action when that title is still available.
- Use current authorized results when you return. Restoration has a finite loading budget and stops when you interact.
- Keep normal loading controls available when a title has moved, disappeared or cannot be reached within that budget.

Fresh visits remain normal Watchlists entries. This patch preserves filters, native history, deletion protection, approval permissions and personal data formats.
