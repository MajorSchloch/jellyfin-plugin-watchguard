# WatchGuard v0.7.11 — native detail actions and Watchlists controls

- Watchlist actions fit Jellyfin’s detail action row, with visible keyboard focus and a clear plus or minus icon. Saved titles remain visibly accented without hovering.
- Watchlists filters use Jellyfin’s native checkbox and field presentation. Labels are easier to read, and the filter panel follows the selected theme.
- Save and Reset keep keyboard focus while busy and ignore repeated or conflicting clicks. Errors remain readable and actions can be retried.

Filtering, saved views, navigation, playback activity and deletion protection retain their existing behavior. This patch does not redesign cards or deletion dialogs.
