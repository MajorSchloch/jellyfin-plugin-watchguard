# WatchGuard v0.7.4 — Watchlists startup handoff

Reloading or opening a saved Watchlists link now shows “Loading Watchlists…” while Jellyfin restores authentication and loads WatchGuard. The authenticated Watchlists view mounts immediately, without briefly showing Home. The temporary state is limited to the exact Watchlists route and releases on navigation, failed loading or a 20-second timeout.

Watchlists Refresh, filters, history and saved preferences retain their existing behavior. Jellyfin Server/Web 12.2.0 and 12.3.0 remain supported with ABI 12.2.0.0 and .NET 10.

See the [startup qualification and limitations](https://github.com/MajorSchloch/jellyfin-plugin-watchguard/blob/master/docs/startup-validation-v074.md).
